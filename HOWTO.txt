=== Wifi

The percentage formula is `min(100, max(0, 2 * (dbm + 100)))`:

| dBm | % | Quality | Tray colour |
|-----|---|---------|-------------|
| ≥ −55 | ≥ 90% | Excellent | Green |
| ≥ −65 | ≥ 70% | Good | Yellow-green |
| ≥ −75 | ≥ 50% | Fair | Dark orange |
| < −75 | < 50% | Poor | Red |
| −100 | 0% | — | Grey (no WiFi) |
